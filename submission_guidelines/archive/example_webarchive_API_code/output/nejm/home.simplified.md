Why NEJM?
---------

Since 1812, NEJM has worked with authors to publish the highest quality evidence that advances medical science and improves patient care.

Benefit from the Highest Standard in Medical Publishing
-------------------------------------------------------

With a commitment to publishing only the highest quality research, NEJM is among the most trusted and authoritative journals in clinical medicine. Our discerning and unbiased peer-review editorial process sets the standard for medical publishing. Your paper will benefit from the careful attention of [esteemed physician editors](/web/20240105050229/https://www.nejm.org/about-nejm/editors-and-publishers) and reviewers, statistical experts, and professional editing, graphics, design, and production personnel.

Have an Impact on the Global Medical Community
----------------------------------------------

Published in NEJM, your research can have an important impact on global medical practice. All NEJM research articles are made freely available six months after publication in a print issue, and NEJM also grants immediate free access to low-income countries through participation in the [Research4Life's Hinari program](https://web.archive.org/web/20240105050229/https://www.who.int/hinari/en/). The NEJM 2022 Journal Impact Factor is 158.5, making NEJM a top-ranking medical journal in the general medicine category. \[Source: 2022 Journal Impact Factor, _Journal Citation Reports_ (Clarivate, 2023)\]. NEJM offers a [Rapid Review](/web/20240105050229/https://www.nejm.org/author-center/request-a-rapid-review) Service that coordinates timing of online publication with presentation at medical conferences, in addition to the extensive reach of NEJM to millions of readers in more than 175 countries.

Ensure Worldwide Reach and Visibility for Your Work
---------------------------------------------------

Articles appearing in NEJM attract extensive media coverage, which we support by offering [prepublication (embargoed) access](/web/20240105050229/https://www.nejm.org/author-center/embargo-policy) to leading medical journalists worldwide. NEJM also actively promotes new content each week to its broad global audience, including more than two million followers of our social media channels. With [Article Metrics](/web/20240105050229/https://www.nejm.org/about-nejm/article-metrics), authors and readers can easily track page views, citations, media coverage, and social media conversations taking place around specific NEJM articles.

Influence Patient Care and Medical Practice
-------------------------------------------

NEJM wants submissions of original research, reviews, case reports, and commentaries that improve patient care and clinical practice or offer new information about the biology of disease. All manuscripts are examined by our [Executive Deputy Editor and senior editorial team](/web/20240105050229/https://www.nejm.org/about-nejm/editors-and-publishers), who consider each submission for originality, quality, strong results, and ethical values.

Submission, revision, and tracking of all manuscripts and related materials is managed through the [NEJM online submission system](https://web.archive.org/web/20240105050229/https://mc05.manuscriptcentral.com/nejm).
