## For Authors

Back to Top
Please read the Duke University Press (DUP) ethics statement before submitting: https://www.dukeupress.edu/journals/journal-authors-editors/ethics-statement-for-journals.

 Submission of a paper to the *Duke Mathematical Journal* acknowledges that the paper is original and is not submitted elsewhere. Authors must make standard copyright assignments once the paper has been accepted. 

In regard to the use of AI, authors should submit work that properly cites or quotes the work of others. DUP will not accept work substantially written by generative AI and Large Language Models (LLMs). Authors must disclose the use of AI or LLMs in their work in a general statement at the beginning of their article (for instance, in an unnumbered note). In addition, each specific use of AI and LLM tools in text, images, tables, or other parts of the work must be cited—just as any external source would be.

 Authors should submit their papers online using the EditFlow manuscript peer-review system at https://ef.msp.org/submit/duke. In order to help us create a more level playing field and get a more accurate idea of the relative lengths of submissions, we ask that your submission be typeset in 11-point font using the amslatex class file (or equivalent) with US letter page and standard margins.

                \documentclass[11pt,letterpaper]{amsart}

 (Please do not use any of the packages fullpage, geometry, anysize, typearea, koma-script, etc. to override the latex defaults.) If you have done this correctly, the text area should measure 5 in by 8 in (about 12.7 cm by 20.3 cm). Please check this before submitting.

 Please be prepared to provide the following when submitting a paper to *DMJ*: 

- A PDF of the manuscript.
- 2020 Mathematics Subject Classifications. If there are primary and secondary numbers, please indicate them.
- An abstract of no more than 300 words. We prefer that the abstract be submitted as part of the paper. Papers written in French should include versions of the abstract in both English and French.
-  In addition to a caption, each figure and image requires *alt text* , a short description that allows nonsighted persons to access a publication’s visual content.
  - If accepted be prepared to include a Word document listing all the alt text descriptions in your article and their page numbers.
  - Resources: Accessible Latex and Making Accessible Documents
- At acceptance, a copy of the manuscript in LaTeX2e, as well as all figures, style files, and any other supplemental files needed to process the paper.
- Grant information for authors where applicable.
- A running head (a shortened version of the title) of no more than 50 characters, including spaces.

 Color figures and images may be used in the online version of the article at no charge. If the use of color is essential for the print version, however, then authors will be required to pay a fee. Contact the *DMJ* production office at dmj@dukeupress.edu for further information. There are no article processing charges.

  

 Please do not upload a revision of a previously submitted article. Instead, use the status link from an email you will receive at submission. Withdraw your paper only if you wish to **completely** remove your paper from consideration by this journal. For initial corrections, use the button to upload a revision. If the button to upload a revision is not available, please contact the journal by using the “Send a Message” button and ask that a revision be requested.

 You can also send an email to dmj@math.duke.edu or editflow@msp.org for assistance.

**Individual Access Instructions**

You should now have access to the

*Duke Mathematical Journal*through Project Euclid. In order to activate your online access to the

*Duke Mathematical Journal*, follow the steps:

 1. Visit projecteuclid.org and sign in.

 2. If you do not already have a projecteuclid.org account, select “Create an account” from within the “Sign In” window.

 3. Complete the form to create a Euclid account.

 4. The e-mail address provided will be used to contact you regarding electronic access. 

If you have any questions, please contact us at subscriptions@dukeupress.edu.

**Institutional Access Instructions**

 Before orders for either *DMJ 100* or the electronic version of the *Duke Mathematical Journal* can be processed, Duke University Press must receive prepayment and have a signed site license agreement on file, unless other arrangements have been made.  

- Prepayment must be sent to Duke University Press Customer Service.

- A signed site license agreement with appropriate IP addresses and customer number must be sent to the attention of the library relations manager at 919-680-6078 or mailed to 905 W. Main St., Suite 18B, Durham, NC 27701.

- Institutions will receive a confirmation e-mail when access is activated.
