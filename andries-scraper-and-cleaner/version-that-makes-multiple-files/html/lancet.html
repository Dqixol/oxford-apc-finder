Submission guidelines

Preparing your manuscript: introduction

Before beginning your author journey with us, we recommend you prepare your manuscript and related forms. This will save you valuable time and ensure your experience is as seamless as possible.

Our submission guidelines will provide you with the information, forms, and resources you need to correctly submit your manuscript.

Select a journal

Choose a journal from the menu for all the guidelines, forms, and relevant information you’ll need to submit your manuscript. If you are unsure or haven’t made a final decision on which journal to submit to, you can learn more about our journals.

Outline

Content types

Ensure we publish the content type you intend to submit, and that it adheres to our guidelines.

View our content types page for more information.

Guidelines

To help ensure your manuscript is ready for submission, please reference the following:  

Download our Information for Authors PDF

Additional guidelines:

  • Formatting and artwork guidelines (PDF)
  • Randomised Controlled Trials (RCT) guidelines (PDF)
  • Systematic reviews and meta-analyses guidelines (PDF)
  • Observational studies guidelines (PDF)
  • Equitable Partnership Declaration FAQs and example form (PDF)
  • Data on sex and gender, and race and ethnicity FAQ
  • Corrections guidelines (PDF)

Standard submission forms

Download and complete the forms, uploading a signed copy with your submission. Please note that The Lancet Group accepts hand-signed and electronic (typewritten) signatures.

Download the Author Statement Forms PDF
Download the ICMJE conflicts of interest form (Word doc)
Download the Equitable Partnership Declaration form (Word doc)

The following documents, if relevant, can be scanned and submitted electronically with your manuscript:

  • Statements of role, if any, of medical writer or editor
  • Acknowledgments—written consent of cited individual
  • Personal communications—written consent of cited individual
  • Use of copyright-protected material—signed permission statements from author and publisher

Article templates

These templates are optional and provided for guidance only. Authors should always refer to the Information for Authors when preparing their manuscript for submission. Use of these templates does not influence editorial assessment, peer review, or publication decisions.

Download the General Article template (Word doc)
Download the Observational Studies template (Word doc)
Download the Randomised Controlled Trial (RCT) template (Word doc)
Download the Systematic Review and Meta-Analysis template (Word doc)

Submit your manuscript

Once you are ready to submit your manuscript to The Lancet, please click on the submit button. This will take you through to Editorial Manager, the journal's manuscript submission system, where you’ll be able to upload your manuscript and additional documentation.

Submit your manuscript Opens in new window  

Pathways to publication

If your manuscript is not accepted by the Lancet journal to which you originally submit, it may be eligible to be transferred to another Lancet or relevant Elsevier journal. Our collaborative editorial teams can provide expert guidance to help you find the best home for your research. If you agree to have your manuscript transferred to another journal, you will not need to reformat your manuscript, and where relevant, the original peer review comments may also be transferred.

If you choose to accept the transfer to another Lancet journal and have already submitted the Author Statement and ICMJE forms with your initial submission, please complete and include this form along with your transferred manuscript:

Download the authorisation to transfer author statement and ICMJE form (PDF)
  • For more information, explore The Lancet Group’s pathways to publication.

Contact our editorial office

We recognise you have options, and we never take your contributions or time for granted. If you have any queries, we will be pleased to help. Please contact our editorial office.
